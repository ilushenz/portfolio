Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

This privacy policy describes how Ilia Chapchakhov ("I", "me") handles data in connection with personal productivity tools that access Google Workspace APIs, including Gmail. These tools are used solely by me for my own accounts and are not offered to the public.

What Data Is Accessed

The application accesses Google account data limited to the scopes authorized during sign-in. This may include Gmail message content, labels, and metadata, used to read and manage my own email.

How Data Is Used

Data is accessed solely to provide email functionality for my own personal and academic use. No data is used for advertising, marketing, analytics, or any purpose unrelated to the application's core functionality.

Data Sharing

No Google user data is shared with, sold to, or disclosed to any third parties, including advertisers, data brokers, or information resellers. Data is not used to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models, for targeted or personalized advertising, or for credit or lending determinations.

Data Storage and Security

OAuth credentials (refresh tokens) are stored locally on my personal devices in encrypted form. No Google user data is stored on remote servers. Access is protected by device-level authentication and encryption.

Data Retention and Deletion

The application does not independently store copies of Google user data beyond what is necessary for immediate use. OAuth tokens can be revoked at any time through Google Account permissions, which immediately terminates the application's access.
